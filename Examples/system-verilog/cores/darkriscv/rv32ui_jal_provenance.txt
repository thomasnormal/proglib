The image is loaded at reset address zero by rv32ui_jal_tb.sv. The testbench
uses the unchanged DarkRISCV source and lowers instruction acknowledgement on
deterministic falling-edge slots. The image has no data-memory operation, so
this row exercises instruction stalls and JAL control flow only. The `ld_st`
image covers the data-stall case separately.
