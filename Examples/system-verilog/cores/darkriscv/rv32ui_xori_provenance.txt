The image is loaded at reset address zero by rv32ui_xori_tb.sv. The testbench
uses the unchanged DarkRISCV source and lowers instruction acknowledgement on
deterministic falling-edge slots. The image has no data-memory operation, so
this row exercises instruction stalls and XORI/immediate operations only. The
`ld_st` image covers the data-stall case separately.
