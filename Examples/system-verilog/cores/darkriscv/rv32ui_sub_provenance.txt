The image is loaded at reset address zero by rv32ui_sub_tb.sv. The testbench
uses the unchanged DarkRISCV source and lowers instruction acknowledgement on
deterministic falling-edge slots. The image itself has no data-memory operation,
so this row exercises instruction stalls only. The load/store image covers the
data-stall case separately.
